## Biography

I received the Ph.D. degree in Computational Mathematics from the School of Mathematical Sciences, Dalian University of Technology, Dalian, China, in 2009. I am currently a Researcher at Zhejiang Lab, Hangzhou, China. My research interests include multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, and embodied intelligence. I have authored more than 80 journal and conference papers in these areas, including publications in IEEE TKDE, IEEE TSMC: Systems, CVPR, ACM MM, and ECCV.

## Research Interests

- **Multi-Agent Systems:** cognition and decision-making mechanisms for satellite agents under incomplete information, continual learning, and multi-agent collaboration.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** action-curiosity-driven learning enhancement, dynamic obstacle avoidance, and path planning in nondeterministic environments.
- **Embodied Intelligence:** cognition-driven autonomous planning and decision-making, including embodied intelligent satellites and spacecraft.
